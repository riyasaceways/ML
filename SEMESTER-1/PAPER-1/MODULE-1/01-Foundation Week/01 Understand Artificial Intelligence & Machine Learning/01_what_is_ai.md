# What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of building computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks can include:

- Understanding language
- Recognizing images
- Finding patterns
- Making predictions
- Making decisions
- Solving problems
- Learning from data

For example:

A normal calculator follows explicitly programmed rules:

`2 + 2 → 4`

An AI system might be given many examples of handwritten numbers and learn to recognize whether an image contains a `2`, `4`, or another number.


## Important distinction

AI is the broad field.

Machine Learning (ML) is one approach used to build AI systems.

Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning.


Therefore:
AI
└── Machine Learning
    └── Deep Learning

AI = the broad goal/field
