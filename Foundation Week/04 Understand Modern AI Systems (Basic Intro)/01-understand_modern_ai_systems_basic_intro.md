# Understand Modern AI Systems (Basic Intro)

### a. Traditional ML Systems

Traditional machine learning systems use algorithms to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions. They usually depend on structured or prepared data and clearly defined features. Examples include predicting house prices, detecting spam emails, recommending products, or identifying fraudulent transactions. These systems are generally designed for a specific task and do not automatically generate new content like modern generative AI systems.

---

### b. Deep Learning Systems

Deep learning is a type of machine learning that uses **artificial neural networks with multiple layers** to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data. Deep learning systems are particularly useful for tasks involving images, audio, video, and language. For example, they can recognize objects in images, understand speech, translate languages, or identify patterns in medical images. Deep learning is therefore a powerful approach within the broader field of machine learning.

---

### c. Generative AI Systems

Generative AI systems are AI systems designed to **generate new content** based on patterns learned from existing data. Depending on the system, they can generate text, images, audio, video, or computer code. For example, a generative AI system can create an article from a prompt, generate an image from a description, or produce code based on a programming requirement. Unlike traditional predictive systems, their main purpose is not only to classify or predict but also to create.

---

### d. Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models, or **LLMs**, are AI models trained on very large amounts of text data to learn patterns in human language. They can process and generate text, answer questions, summarize information, translate languages, explain concepts, and assist with programming. LLMs do not simply store complete answers and retrieve them; they learn statistical patterns in language and use those patterns to generate responses based on the input they receive.

---

### e. Agentic AI Systems

Agentic AI systems are AI systems designed to **perform tasks through multiple steps rather than simply responding to a single request**. An agent may interpret a goal, decide what actions are needed, use available tools, observe the results, and continue working toward the goal. For example, an AI agent could research a topic, organize the information, write a report, and revise it based on the results. The key idea is that the system can take actions toward a goal with some degree of autonomy.
