# Real-world applications of AI systems

## Real-world applications of AI systems

AI systems are used in many areas to help humans make decisions, automate tasks, recognize patterns, and solve problems.

Examples include:

- Farming — crop and soil monitoring
- Banking — fraud detection and risk analysis
- Education — personalized learning and tutoring
- Healthcare — medical image analysis and decision support
- Transportation — navigation and autonomous driving
- Monitoring — detecting unusual events or system problems



AI can provide fast and consistent decisions based on defined 
objectives and large amounts of information, without being 
affected by human emotions. However, AI can still make wrong 
decisions, and humans are responsible for defining its 
objectives and constraints.
